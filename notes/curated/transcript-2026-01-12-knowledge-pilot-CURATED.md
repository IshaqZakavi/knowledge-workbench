## Context

The pilot asks whether a reviewer can trace recommendations to their evidence.
The output should preserve decisions, the reasons behind them, ownership limits
and open questions. A shorter note is not automatically a better note.

## Key points

- Markdown notes and PDF text are the initial input types.
- Audio requires separate consent, retention and speaker-attribution decisions.
- Rowan requested a concrete example before the final structure is chosen.
  That is one observed request, not proof of a permanent working style.
- Rowan's review commitment is limited to this pilot.
- There is no measured productivity outcome.

## Decisions

- Start the pilot with Markdown and extracted PDF text.
- Defer audio until recording permission and speaker review are defined.
- Preserve unresolved questions in the decision brief.
