When a musical idea is still being explored, preserve the ability to change one
part without rebuilding the whole arrangement. Separate stems are one practical
way to do that. They add organization overhead, so they are a tradeoff rather
than a rule for every project.

This is a generalization from one fictional session. It is not proof that this
workflow improves every production or saves a measured amount of time.
