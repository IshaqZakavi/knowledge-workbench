# Selective Atoms

Selected adaptation of the knowledge-work method for this public template.

Start with reviewed curated material and search existing atomic notes before creating another.

Extract only a cross-context idea with an explanation of why it matters and where it may fail. Routine status, configuration details, stakeholder preferences and individual task updates usually stay in curated notes. Zero atoms is a normal result; the original method expects at most a few per source.

Give each note one clear idea, a stable identity and a meaningful filename. Keep it brief, normally under 60 lines including frontmatter. Use concept tags rather than a project tag shared by everything. Cite the curated origin and preserve caveats. Do not manufacture a general principle from one incident.

For a proposed principle, distinguish source agreement from the owner's confidence. Record a counterexample or limitation when known. Human review is required before treating a model-drafted principle as the owner's settled view.

The demonstration's `decision-context` record is an authored fictional example. Its presence does not show that an agent autonomously discovered or validated the principle.
